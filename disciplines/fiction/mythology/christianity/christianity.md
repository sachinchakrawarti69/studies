# Christianity

## Overview

**Christianity** is the world's largest religion and one of the three major Abrahamic religions. It is centered on the life, teachings, death, and resurrection of **Jesus Christ**, whom Christians believe is the Messiah (Christ) and the Son of God. Christianity teaches belief in one God, salvation through Jesus Christ, love of God and neighbor, forgiveness, and eternal life.

Followers of Christianity are called **Christians**.

# Etymology

The word **Christian** comes from the Greek word **Christos**, meaning **"Anointed One"** or **"Messiah."**

Followers of Jesus were first called Christians in **Antioch** (Acts 11:26).

---

# Historical Background

## Jewish Origins

Christianity emerged within the Jewish community of the 1st century CE.

According to the New Testament:

- Jesus was born in Bethlehem.
- He was raised in Nazareth.
- He preached throughout Judea and Galilee.
- He gathered disciples and taught about the Kingdom of God.

---

## Ministry of Jesus

Jesus emphasized:

- Love
- Mercy
- Forgiveness
- Repentance
- Faith
- Compassion
- Justice

He taught through sermons, conversations, and parables.

---

## Crucifixion and Resurrection

According to Christian belief:

- Jesus was crucified under Roman authority.
- He died on the cross.
- He rose from the dead on the third day.
- His resurrection is the foundation of Christian faith.

---

## Early Church

Following the resurrection:

- Jesus' disciples spread Christianity throughout the Roman Empire.
- Apostles such as Peter and Paul played major roles in the growth of the Church.
- Christian communities were established across the Mediterranean world.

---

# Core Beliefs

- One God
- Jesus Christ as the Messiah
- Resurrection of Jesus
- Salvation

- Love of neighbor
- Forgiveness
- Eternal life

# The Trinity

Most Christian denominations believe in the **Trinity**, the doctrine that God exists as one divine being in three persons:

- God the Father
- God the Son (Jesus Christ)
- God the Holy Spirit

Some Christian groups hold different theological views regarding the Trinity.

---

# Jesus Christ

According to Christianity, Jesus is:

- Son of God
- Messiah (Christ)
- Savior
- Teacher
- Lord

His life and teachings form the foundation of Christian belief.

Different Christian traditions recognize different numbers of sacraments.

Two observed by nearly all Christians are:

## Baptism

Symbolizes entry into the Christian faith.

---

## Communion (Eucharist)

Commemorates Jesus' Last Supper with his disciples.

# Major Denominations

## Roman Catholic Church

- Largest Christian denomination.
- Led by the Pope.
- Recognizes seven sacraments.

---

## Eastern Orthodoxy

- Composed of self-governing churches.
- Emphasizes ancient liturgy and tradition.

---

## Protestantism

Originated during the 16th-century Reformation.

Includes many denominations, such as:

- Lutheran
- Anglican
- Baptist
- Methodist
- Presbyterian
- Pentecostal
- Reformed

---

# Major Festivals

## Christmas

Celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ.

Observed on **25 December** by many Christians, though some churches follow different calendars.

---

## Easter

Celebrates the resurrection of Jesus.

Considered the most important festival in Christianity.

---

## Good Friday

Commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus.

---

## Palm Sunday

Marks Jesus' entry into Jerusalem.

---

## Pentecost

Celebrates the coming of the Holy Spirit upon the apostles.

# Summary

Christianity is a monotheistic Abrahamic religion centered on the life, teachings, death, and resurrection of Jesus Christ. It teaches love of God and neighbor, forgiveness, faith, and hope in eternal life. Through its diverse traditions, Christianity has profoundly influenced the history, culture, philosophy, art, education, and societies of many parts of the world.
