# Comprehensive Accounting Structure with NCERT Curriculum 
 
## Overview 
This comprehensive accounting structure covers: 
 
- **Class 11 NCERT** - Financial Accounting I and II 
- **Class 12 NCERT** - Company Accounts and Cash Flow 
- **Advanced Financial Accounting** - AS, IND AS, IFRS 
- **Cost Accounting** - Complete cost concepts and methods 
- **Managerial Accounting** - Budgeting, decision making, performance 
- **Advanced Topics** - Partnership, Government, Forensic, Modeling 
- **Practical Cases** - Real-world problems and projects 
- **Question Bank** - Chapter-wise questions and mock tests 
- **Exam Preparation** - Revision notes and exam strategies 
 
## How to Use 
1. Start with Class 11 NCERT (01_class_11_ncert) 
2. Progress to Class 12 NCERT (02_class_12_ncert) 
3. Move to Advanced topics (03-06) 
4. Practice with Questions and Projects (07-10) 
 
## Career Paths 
- Chartered Accountancy (CA) 
- Cost and Management Accountancy (CMA) 
- Company Secretary (CS) 
- Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) 
- MBA Finance 
- Financial Modeling and Analysis
