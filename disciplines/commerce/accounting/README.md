# Accounting Study Repository

## Overview
This repository contains a comprehensive collection of accounting study materials organized by class, topic, and difficulty level.

## Folder Structure

### 01_class_11_ncert
NCERT Accounting curriculum for Class 11 students.
- **Part A**: Financial Accounting I (Chapters 1-8)
- **Part B**: Financial Accounting II (Chapters 9-12)

### 02_class_12_ncert
NCERT Accounting curriculum for Class 12 students.
- **Part A**: Company Accounts (Chapters 1-7)
- **Part B**: Cash Flow Statement (Chapters 8-9)

### 03_financial_accounting_advanced
Advanced topics in financial accounting:
- Accounting Standards (AS 1-28)
- Ind AS and IFRS
- Consolidated Financial Statements
- Amalgamation and Restructuring
- Liquidation Accounting

### 04_cost_accounting
Complete cost accounting coverage:
- Cost Basics
- Material, Labour, and Overhead Cost Control
- Methods of Costing
- Activity Based Costing
- Marginal Costing
- Standard Costing and Variance Analysis

### 05_managerial_accounting
Management accounting topics:
- Basics of Managerial Accounting
- Budgeting and Forecasting
- Decision Making Tools
- Performance Management

### 06_advanced_accounting_topics
Specialized accounting areas:
- Partnership Accounting
- Government Accounting
- Forensic Accounting
- Financial Modeling
- Ethics in Accounting
- International Accounting

### 07_practical_cases_and_projects
Hands-on practice materials:
- Class 11 & 12 Practical Problems
- Case Studies
- Projects

### 08_reference_materials
Useful references:
- Formula Sheets
- Accounting Software Guides
- Career Resources

### 09_question_bank
Practice questions:
- Chapter-wise MCQs
- Short and Long Answer Questions
- Competitive Exam Questions
- Mock Tests

### 10_exam_preparation
Exam-focused materials:
- Revision Notes
- Important Questions
- Sample Papers
- Study Tips

## How to Use This Repository

1. **Students**: Start with your class level and work through chapters systematically
2. **Professionals**: Focus on advanced topics and case studies relevant to your field
3. **Exam Aspirants**: Use the question bank and exam preparation sections

## Contributing

Feel free to add notes, solve problems, and share resources with fellow learners.
