# Chapter 01: Introduction to Derivatives

---

## 1. A derivative is a financial contract whose value is derived from:

A) Company profit  
B) Government policy  
C) Underlying asset  
D) Bank interest rate  

**Answer:** C) Underlying asset

**Explanation:**  
The value of a derivative depends on another asset called the underlying asset.

---

## 2. Which of the following is an example of an underlying asset?

A) Futures contract  
B) Option contract  
C) Gold  
D) Swap agreement  

**Answer:** C) Gold

**Explanation:**  
Gold can be an underlying asset for gold futures and options.

---

## 3. Which statement correctly describes derivatives?

A) They have independent value  
B) Their value depends on another asset  
C) They are only used by banks  
D) They cannot be traded  

**Answer:** B) Their value depends on another asset

---

## 4. The original purpose of derivatives was:

A) Gambling  
B) Risk management  
C) Tax reduction  
D) Currency printing  

**Answer:** B) Risk management

---

## 5. A farmer selling futures to protect crop prices is an example of:

A) Speculation  
B) Arbitrage  
C) Hedging  
D) Market making  

**Answer:** C) Hedging

---

# Types of Derivatives

---

## 6. Which is NOT a major type of derivative?

A) Futures  
B) Options  
C) Bonds  
D) Swaps  

**Answer:** C) Bonds

---

## 7. A forward contract is:

A) Exchange traded and standardized  
B) Customized agreement between parties  
C) Only used by governments  
D) A stock market index  

**Answer:** B) Customized agreement between parties

---

## 8. Futures contracts are mainly traded on:

A) Private agreements  
B) Regulated exchanges  
C) Banks only  
D) Insurance companies  

**Answer:** B) Regulated exchanges

---

## 9. An option provides:

A) Guaranteed profit  
B) Right but not obligation  
C) Mandatory buying only  
D) Ownership of company  

**Answer:** B) Right but not obligation

---

## 10. The two main types of options are:

A) Future and Forward  
B) Call and Put  
C) Stock and Bond  
D) Spot and Swap  

**Answer:** B) Call and Put

---

# Uses of Derivatives

---

## 11. Hedging is used to:

A) Increase risk  
B) Reduce financial risk  
C) Avoid all losses  
D) Create companies  

**Answer:** B) Reduce financial risk

---

## 12. A trader buying Nifty Futures expecting market rise is:

A) Hedger  
B) Speculator  
C) Arbitrageur  
D) Regulator  

**Answer:** B) Speculator

---

## 13. Arbitrage means:

A) Holding assets forever  
B) Profit from price differences  
C) Reducing taxes  
D) Issuing shares  

**Answer:** B) Profit from price differences

---

## 14. Portfolio managers use derivatives mainly for:

A) Risk control  
B) Company registration  
C) Accounting entries  
D) Employee hiring  

**Answer:** A) Risk control

---

# Market Participants

---

## 15. Who uses derivatives to reduce existing risk?

A) Speculators  
B) Hedgers  
C) Arbitrageurs  
D) Brokers  

**Answer:** B) Hedgers

---

## 16. A person who earns from market price differences is called:

A) Hedger  
B) Arbitrageur  
C) Investor  
D) Auditor  

**Answer:** B) Arbitrageur

---

## 17. Market makers help markets by providing:

A) Taxes  
B) Liquidity  
C) Loans  
D) Insurance  

**Answer:** B) Liquidity

---

# Exchange Traded vs OTC

---

## 18. Exchange-traded derivatives are:

A) Customized privately  
B) Standardized contracts  
C) Without regulation  
D) Illegal contracts  

**Answer:** B) Standardized contracts

---

## 19. OTC derivatives generally have higher:

A) Liquidity  
B) Counterparty risk  
C) Regulation  
D) Transparency  

**Answer:** B) Counterparty risk

---

# Risk Management

---

## 20. Leverage in derivatives can:

A) Remove all risk  
B) Increase both profit and loss potential  
C) Guarantee returns  
D) Prevent trading  

**Answer:** B) Increase both profit and loss potential

---

# Assertion and Reason

---

## 21. Assertion:

Derivatives help businesses manage price risk.

Reason:

Derivatives allow participants to lock future prices.

A) Both are true and reason explains assertion  
B) Both are false  
C) Assertion true, reason false  
D) Assertion false, reason true  

**Answer:** A

---

# True or False

---

## 22. Futures contracts are standardized agreements.

**Answer:** True

---

## 23. Options buyers have unlimited loss.

**Answer:** False

**Explanation:**  
Option buyers generally lose only the premium paid.

---

## 24. Derivatives always reduce risk.

**Answer:** False

**Explanation:**  
Derivatives can also increase risk when used incorrectly.
