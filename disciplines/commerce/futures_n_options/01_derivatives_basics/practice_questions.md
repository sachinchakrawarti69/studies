## 11. Futures Profit Calculation

A trader buys Nifty Futures at:

Entry Price = 22,000

Exit Price = 22,500

Lot Size = 50

Calculate:

1. Profit per unit
2. Total profit

Formula:

```
Profit = (Selling Price - Buying Price) × Lot Size
```
