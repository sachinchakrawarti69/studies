# Derivatives Glossary

> **Module:** Derivatives Basics  
> **Purpose:** Quick reference of important terms used in derivatives markets.

---

# A

## Arbitrage

The process of earning profit from price differences of the same asset in different markets.

**Example:**

Gold price:

| Market | Price |
|---|---:|
| Exchange A | ₹60,000 |
| Exchange B | ₹60,500 |

Buying from the cheaper market and selling in the expensive market creates an arbitrage opportunity.

---

## Arbitrageur

A market participant who identifies and exploits price differences.

---

## Asset

Anything that has economic value.

Examples:

- Stocks
- Bonds
- Commodities
- Currency
- Indices

---

# B

## Basis

The difference between the spot price and futures price.

Formula:

```
Basis = Spot Price - Futures Price
```

---

## Basis Risk

The risk that the difference between spot and futures prices changes unexpectedly.

---

# C

## Call Option

An option contract that gives the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy an asset at a specified price.

---

## Clearing House

An institution responsible for settling trades and managing counterparty risk.

---

## Commodity Derivative

A derivative contract whose underlying asset is a commodity.

Examples:

- Gold Futures
- Crude Oil Futures
- Wheat Futures

---

## Counterparty Risk

The risk that the other party in a contract fails to fulfill its obligation.

Common in:

- Forward contracts
- OTC derivatives

---

# D

## Derivative

A financial contract whose value is derived from an underlying asset.

Examples:

- Futures
- Options
- Swaps
- Forwards

---

## Derivatives Market

A financial market where derivative contracts are bought and sold.

---

# E

## Exchange-Traded Derivative

A standardized derivative contract traded on a regulated exchange.

Examples:

- NSE Futures
- CME Futures

Features:

- Standardized contracts
- High liquidity
- Lower counterparty risk

---

# F

## Forward Contract

A customized agreement between two parties to buy or sell an asset at a future date.

Features:

- Private agreement
- OTC market
- Customized terms

---

## Futures Contract

A standardized agreement traded on an exchange to buy or sell an asset at a future date.

Features:

- Exchange traded
- Daily settlement
- Margin requirement

---

# H

## Hedging

A strategy used to reduce financial risk caused by price movements.

Examples:

- Farmer protecting crop prices
- Airline protecting fuel costs
- Exporter protecting currency risk

---

## Hedger

A person or organization using derivatives to reduce risk.

---

# L

## Leverage

The ability to control a large position using a smaller amount of capital.

Example:

A trader controls ₹10 lakh worth of futures with ₹1 lakh margin.

Leverage increases both:

- Potential profit
- Potential loss

---

## Liquidity

The ease with which an asset can be bought or sold without affecting its price significantly.

---

# M

## Margin

The amount of money deposited with a broker or exchange to open a derivatives position.

Types:

- Initial Margin
- Maintenance Margin

---

## Market Maker

A participant who provides buying and selling prices to improve market liquidity.

---

# O

## Option

A derivative contract that gives the buyer a right but not an obligation.

Types:

1. Call Option
2. Put Option

---

## Option Buyer

The party who purchases an option and receives the right.

Maximum loss:

Premium paid

---

## Option Seller (Writer)

The party who sells an option and receives premium.

Obligation:

Must fulfill contract requirements if exercised.

---

## OTC (Over The Counter)

Derivative contracts traded privately between parties.

Examples:

- Currency swaps
- Interest rate swaps

---

# P

## Premium

The price paid by an option buyer to purchase an option contract.

---

## Put Option

An option giving the buyer the right to sell an asset at a specified price.

---

# S

## Speculation

Taking market risk with the expectation of earning profit from price movements.

Example:

Buying Nifty Futures expecting market rise.

---

## Speculator

A trader who accepts risk to earn potential profit.

---

## Spot Price

The current market price of an asset.

---

## Swap

A derivative contract where two parties exchange future cash flows.

Examples:

- Interest Rate Swap
- Currency Swap

---

# U

## Underlying Asset

The asset from which a derivative gets its value.

Examples:

| Derivative | Underlying |
|-|-|
| Nifty Futures | Nifty 50 Index |
| Gold Futures | Gold |
| USDINR Futures | Currency |

---

# V

## Volatility

The degree of price movement of an asset over time.

High volatility means:

- Larger price changes
- Higher risk
- Higher option premiums

---

# Important Formula Terms

| Term | Formula |
|-|-|
| Basis | Spot Price - Futures Price |
| Profit/Loss Futures | Selling Price - Buying Price |
| Option Payoff (Call) | Max(0, Spot - Strike) |
| Option Payoff (Put) | Max(0, Strike - Spot) |
