# Why Do Women Have Menstruation?

## Introduction

**Menstruation** (also called a **period**) is a natural biological process that occurs in females of reproductive age. It is the monthly shedding of the **uterine lining (endometrium)** when pregnancy does not occur.

Menstruation is a normal part of the human reproductive system and plays an important role in fertility.

---

# What Is Menstruation?

Menstruation is the process where the body removes the thickened inner lining of the uterus along with blood and tissue through the vagina.

The menstrual flow contains:

- Blood
- Uterine lining cells
- Mucus
- Other tissues

A menstrual cycle usually occurs approximately every **21–35 days**, although the length varies between individuals.

---

# Why Does the Body Have Menstruation?

The main purpose of menstruation is related to **human reproduction**.

Each month, the female body prepares for a possible pregnancy:

1. The ovaries release an egg (**ovulation**).
2. Hormones signal the uterus to prepare for pregnancy.
3. The uterine lining becomes thick and rich with nutrients.
4. If the egg becomes fertilized, the embryo can attach to this lining.
5. If fertilization does not happen, the body removes the unused lining.
6. The cycle begins again.

Menstruation is therefore the body's way of resetting the reproductive system for a possible future pregnancy.

---

# The Menstrual Cycle Explained

The menstrual cycle has four main phases:

## 1. Menstrual Phase

**Duration:** Around 3–7 days

During this phase:

- The uterine lining breaks down.
- Blood and tissue leave the body through the vagina.
- Hormone levels are low.

This is what people commonly call a "period."

---

## 2. Follicular Phase

During this phase:

- The brain signals the ovaries to develop an egg.
- Estrogen levels increase.
- The uterine lining begins rebuilding.

---

## 3. Ovulation Phase

Usually occurs around the middle of the cycle.

During ovulation:

- A mature egg is released from the ovary.
- The egg travels through the fallopian tube.
- This is the most fertile period.

---

## 4. Luteal Phase

During this phase:

- The body produces hormones to support possible pregnancy.
- The uterine lining remains thick.

If pregnancy does not occur:

- Hormone levels drop.
- The uterine lining breaks down.
- Menstruation begins again.

---

# Role of Hormones

Menstruation is controlled by several hormones:

## Estrogen

Functions:

- Builds the uterine lining.
- Helps egg development.
- Regulates reproductive changes.

## Progesterone

Functions:

- Maintains the uterine lining.
- Prepares the uterus for pregnancy.

## Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH)

Functions:

- Helps eggs mature inside the ovaries.

## Luteinizing Hormone (LH)

Functions:

- Triggers ovulation.

---

# Why Do Humans Menstruate?

Humans are among the few mammals that experience regular menstruation.

Scientists believe menstruation evolved because:

- Human pregnancies require a highly developed uterine environment.
- The uterus prepares a strong nutrient-rich lining.
- The body removes and rebuilds this lining when pregnancy does not happen.

This process helps create suitable conditions for successful pregnancy.

---

# Why Does Menstruation Cause Bleeding?

The uterus creates a blood-rich lining to support a developing embryo.

When pregnancy does not occur:

- The body no longer needs this extra tissue.
- Hormone levels decrease.
- Small blood vessels break.
- The lining separates and leaves the body.

The bleeding is not an injury; it is a controlled biological process.

---

# Common Menstrual Symptoms

Some people experience:

- Abdominal cramps
- Lower back pain
- Breast tenderness
- Mood changes
- Fatigue
- Headaches
- Food cravings
- Bloating

These symptoms are caused mainly by hormonal changes.

---

# Why Is Menstruation Important?

Menstruation shows that:

- The reproductive system is functioning.
- Hormonal cycles are occurring normally.
- The body is capable of supporting pregnancy.

However, having or not having periods does not determine a person's value or identity.

---

# Menstruation and Health

A healthy menstrual cycle can be influenced by:

- Nutrition
- Exercise
- Stress levels
- Sleep
- Hormonal health
- Medical conditions

Changes such as extremely painful periods, very heavy bleeding, or sudden irregular cycles may require medical attention.

---

# Common Myths About Menstruation

## Myth 1: Menstruation Is Dirty

**Fact:**

Menstrual blood is a normal biological fluid and menstruation is a healthy body process.

---

## Myth 2: Women Are Weak During Periods

**Fact:**

People experience menstruation differently. Some have mild symptoms, while others experience discomfort.

---

## Myth 3: Periods Stop a Person From Normal Activities

**Fact:**

Many people continue normal daily activities, including studying, working, exercising, and playing sports during menstruation.

---

# Menstruation and Human Evolution

Menstruation is connected to:

- Human reproduction
- Evolution of pregnancy
- Hormonal regulation
- Uterine biology

It is an example of how the human body prepares repeatedly for the possibility of creating new life.

---

# Summary

Menstruation occurs because the female reproductive system prepares every month for a possible pregnancy.

When pregnancy does not occur:

- The uterine lining is removed.
- Hormone levels change.
- The menstrual cycle starts again.

It is a natural, healthy, and essential biological process.
