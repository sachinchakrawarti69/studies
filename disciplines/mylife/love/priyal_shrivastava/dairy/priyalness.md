# Visual Memory

> I cannot preserve her photograph,
> so I preserve a memory instead.

If memories could borrow a face,
mine would borrow a little inspiration from Emma Watson.

Not because they were the same,
but because my heart found echoes of a familiar elegance.

In my memory,

- Her eyes reminded me of Emma Watson's gentle gaze.
- Her smile carried a similar warmth.
- Her nose had a graceful shape that brought Emma to my mind.
- Her expressions felt natural and quietly beautiful.
- Her simplicity was what made her unforgettable.

These are not exact comparisons.
They are only the closest picture my words can paint.

She was never Emma.

She was always Priyal.

# About

- Name: Priyal Shrivastava
- Love Started: 2019
- Confession: 2024
- Status: One-sided Love
- Reality: She chose her own path.
- My Responsibility: Respect her choice and continue my own life.

---

# What She Meant To Me

She was not just a girl.

She represented:

- Hope
- Innocence
- Kindness
- Beauty
- Simplicity
- A future I imagined

Some of these qualities belonged to her.

Some belonged to my imagination.

Today I know the difference.

---

# What Was Real

- We talked for months.
- We met in college.
- I genuinely cared about her.
- My feelings were real.

---

# What Was Not Real

- We were never in a relationship.
- We never promised each other a future.
- Marriage was my imagination.
- Forever existed only inside my mind.

---

# My Biggest Mistake

I built a future before reality existed.

I loved the possibility more than the certainty.

---

# What Hurt Me

Not rejection.

Not blocking.

The death of the future I imagined.

---

# Lessons

- Love cannot force love.
- Respect is greater than possession.
- Every person has the right to choose.
- Loving someone does not make them responsible for my happiness.

---

# Rules

- No contact.
- No profile checking.
- No waiting for miracles.
- No interfering in her life.
- Respect her decision.
- Respect myself.

---

# If She Marries Someone Else

I will feel pain.

I may feel jealous.

I may cry.

But I will never interfere.

Her happiness is her decision.

My character is my decision.

---

# What I Want To Remember

She was an important chapter.

She is not my entire book.

---

# My Promise

I will never use love to control someone.

I will never use pain to justify hurting someone.

I will become a man who is remembered for his character, not his obsession.

---

# Goodbye

Thank you for the memories.

Thank you for teaching me what attachment feels like.

I release the future I imagined.

I wish you peace.

Now I must learn to create peace for myself.

---

Sometimes, the lesson becomes the greatest gift."*
