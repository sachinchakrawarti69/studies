# Doomsday Preparation
### Mental Preparation for the Hardest Day

> **Purpose**
>
> This document is not written to make me suffer.
> It is written so that, if the hardest day of my life comes,
> I already know how I want to respond.

---

# Q) What if one day she marries someone else?

## What Will Happen

- It may feel like a dream has ended forever.
- I may feel jealousy, sadness, and emptiness.
- It may feel like the future I imagined no longer exists.
- This may become one of the hardest days of my life.
- I may lose my appetite.
- I may not feel like working.
- I may struggle to sleep that night.
- I may question my life for a while.
- I may cry or I may feel completely numb.
- My mind may replay old memories.
- My heart will hurt.
- **This pain is temporary, even if it feels endless.**

---

## What I Won't Do

- I will not attend her wedding.
- I will not contact her.
- I will not contact her family or friends.
- I will not search for updates about her.
- I will not check her social media.
- I will not interfere in her new life.
- I will not try to change her decision.
- I will not use alcohol, drugs, or other unhealthy ways to escape the pain.
- I will not let my pain become hatred.
- I will not harm myself.
- I will not harm anyone else.
- I will not make any decision that I may regret later.

---

## What I Will Do

- I will allow myself to feel the pain.
- I will not let my emotions control my actions.
- I will respect her decision and her new life.
- I will remind myself that every person has the right to choose their own future.
- I will stay away from social media that day.
- I will go for a walk or exercise.
- I will write my feelings instead of acting on them.
- I will remind myself that this is the end of one chapter, not my entire life.
- I will continue building my own future.
- Tomorrow I will wake up and continue my journey.

---

# Emergency Reminder

If today feels unbearable, remember:

- Pain is temporary.
- Decisions made during emotional storms often become lifelong regrets.
- My character is more important than my emotions.
- I don't need to act on every thought I have.
- Today I only need to survive today.

---

# My Principles

- Love is not ownership.
- Respect is greater than possession.
- Every person has the right to choose their own life.
- My pain is my responsibility.
- Healing takes time.
- I choose dignity over desperation.

---

# Final Promise

> "I cannot control another person's choices.
> I can control my own character."

> "The hardest day of my life will not become the worst decision of my life."

---

**Status:** Prepared, not defeated.
