## Introduction

This page is a chapter of my personal life.

I have admired **Shraddha Kapoor** since **2013**. What began as admiration for an actress gradually became a deep emotional attachment. For many years, she occupied a special place in my thoughts and imagination.

This page is not written to seek attention, a response, or recognition.

It is simply my story.

---

## How It Started

Like many people, I first noticed Shraddha Kapoor through her films.

Over time, I admired her for qualities that seemed genuine:

- Her smile.
- Her simplicity.
- Her calm personality.
- Her kindness.
- Her dedication to her work.
- The positive energy she brings to millions of people.

These qualities stayed with me long after watching her movies.

---

## More Than a Fan

As the years passed, my admiration became emotionally significant.

Even though we have never met and she has never known me personally, my mind built a connection that felt very real to me.

Looking back, I understand that this was a one-sided emotional attachment.

It wasn't a relationship.

It was my own feelings.

---

## What I Learned

This experience taught me many things about myself.

I learned that emotions don't always follow logic.

I learned that admiration can become attachment if we are not careful.

I learned that our minds can hold onto dreams for many years.

Most importantly, I learned that healing begins with accepting reality.

---

## Reality

The reality is simple.

Shraddha Kapoor has her own life.

She has her own dreams, career, family, and relationships.

She does not know me personally.

There is nothing wrong with that.

She never made any promises to me.

My feelings belonged only to me.

Accepting this truth has been one of the hardest lessons of my life.

---

## Gratitude

Despite the emotional challenges, I choose gratitude.

Thank you for unknowingly inspiring me during different phases of my life.

Thank you for making me smile through your work.

Thank you for showing kindness and positivity.

Some people inspire millions without ever knowing it.

---

## Choosing to Move Forward

Today, I choose something different.

I choose reality over fantasy.

I choose growth over attachment.

I choose to build my own life instead of living inside imagined possibilities.

My focus is now on:

- Building my career.
- Improving my health.
- Becoming financially independent.
- Creating meaningful projects.
- Forming real relationships with people who know me personally.
- Becoming the best version of myself.

---

## A Beautiful Chapter

Every life has chapters.

Some chapters are joyful.

Some are painful.

Some teach us lessons we never expected.

For me, admiring Shraddha Kapoor was one of those chapters.

It helped me understand my emotions, my hopes, and my capacity to care deeply.

Now it is time for a new chapter.

---

## Final Words

I sincerely wish Shraddha Kapoor happiness, good health, peace, and continued success.

As for me, I will carry forward the lessons this journey taught me while building my own future.

Some stories are not meant to become relationships.

Sometimes, they simply become memories that shape who we are.
